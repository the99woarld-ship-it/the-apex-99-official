A POSITIVE TRAINING ENVIRONMENT

What Parents Can Look For

We aim to create a welcoming environment where children can learn, practise and grow while parents remain informed about their progress.

01. Trainer-Led Practice

Training activities are intended to be guided by qualified or experienced trainers appropriate to the programme. Trainer assignments can be confirmed during admission.

02. Age-Appropriate Learning

Practice intensity and skill activities should be suitable for participants' ages, experience and development needs.

03. Fitness & Coordination

Activities can focus on balance, movement, agility, coordination and sport-specific fitness in an age-appropriate way.

04. Confidence & Teamwork

Players are encouraged to communicate, cooperate, respect teammates and opponents, and learn through practice.

05. Parent Communication

Parents can ask about training schedules, attendance, programme expectations and progress updates during the admission process.

06. Safety & Supervision

We encourage clear safety procedures, suitable equipment, supervised sessions and an appropriate process for reporting concerns or injuries.

For parents and guardians: Please confirm the actual training venue, trainer credentials, available facilities, schedule, safety arrangements and fees with THE APEX 99 before completing admission. Specific facilities and services depend on the selected programme and venue.
