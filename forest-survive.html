ABOUT THE CHALLENGE

Enter a professionally managed forest-survival experience designed around teamwork, decision-making, practical survival tasks and controlled outdoor challenges. Participants will complete the challenge under event rules and safety supervision.

CHALLENGE 01

🔥 FIRE CHALLENGE

Complete the fire-making challenge only in the designated supervised area and according to event safety rules.

CHALLENGE 02

🪵 SHELTER BUILDING

Build a practical forest shelter using permitted natural materials and demonstrate teamwork and planning.

CHALLENGE 03

🪚 CUTTING TASK

Complete the controlled cutting challenge using event-approved equipment under trained supervision.

CHALLENGE 04

💧 WATER PURIFICATION

Turn collected dirty water into safe drinking water using the approved purification method provided by the event team.

CHALLENGE 05

🌿 FOOD IDENTIFICATION

Identify approved edible food resources in the challenge area. No unknown plant, fruit or food may be consumed.

CHALLENGE 06

🧭 SURVIVAL DECISION

Complete navigation, resource-management and problem-solving tasks as instructed by the challenge team.
