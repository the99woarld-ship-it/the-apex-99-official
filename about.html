ABOUT THE APEX 99

BUILDING EXPERIENCES.
DISCOVERING TALENT.

THE APEX 99 is a professional platform for events, sports, competitions, adventure, entertainment and tours. We create opportunities, experiences and platforms where people can discover their abilities, learn something new, enjoy life and move towards a brighter future.

🏢

WHO WE ARE

THE APEX 99 is a professionally focused event and experience organization, operating as a unit of SMPN RECORD MANAGEMENT . Our work brings together sports, competitions, adventure, entertainment and travel under one platform.

From football and cricket to other sports, talent hunts, competitions, outdoor experiences and curated tours, our goal is to create organized and meaningful opportunities for people across India.

🏆

SPORTS & TALENT DISCOVERY

One of our core purposes is to identify talented people who are genuinely skilled, committed and deserving of an opportunity.

Through football, cricket and other sports programmes, talent hunts and competitions, we aim to provide a professional platform where participants can demonstrate their real ability.

🎪

EVENTS & ENTERTAINMENT

We create and manage events that bring people together through sport, entertainment, competition and shared experiences.

Our objective is to make every event organized, engaging, memorable and professionally presented.

🥇

COMPETITIONS

Competitions are designed to give participants a chance to test their skills, confidence, discipline and ability.

  • Sports competitions
  • Talent hunts
  • Skill-based challenges
  • Recognition and awards
  • Professional evaluation
🌲

ADVENTURE

Our adventure programmes are created to take people beyond their comfort zone and introduce them to new experiences.

Through outdoor challenges and nature-based activities, participants can learn practical skills, teamwork, problem solving and the importance of adapting to the natural environment.

✈️

TOURS & TRAVEL

Travel is not only about visiting a new place. It is also about refreshing the mind, taking a break from everyday life and creating memories with people.

THE APEX 99 organizes travel experiences designed to help participants explore new destinations, enjoy nature, experience different cultures and return with a refreshed mind.

🎯

OUR PURPOSE

We believe that talent exists everywhere, but opportunity does not always reach everyone. THE APEX 99 aims to help bridge that gap by creating platforms where ability can be seen, evaluated and recognized.

Our purpose is not simply to organize an event. We want every programme to have a reason — whether that means discovering a talented player, creating a memorable experience, teaching a new outdoor skill, giving people a chance to compete or simply helping someone refresh their mind through travel.

🚀

OUR MISSION

“To discover genuine talent, create meaningful opportunities and deliver professionally managed experiences across India.”

🌟

OUR VISION

To build a trusted platform where talent gets opportunity, participants get meaningful experiences and every programme creates a positive impact on the people involved.

🇮🇳

OUR VISION FOR INDIA

From a young footballer waiting for an opportunity to a traveller looking for a new experience, from a competitor testing their ability to an adventurer learning to live with nature — THE APEX 99 wants to create platforms that encourage people to participate, learn, perform and grow.

We believe the future becomes brighter when skill meets opportunity, experience meets learning and passion meets the right platform.
