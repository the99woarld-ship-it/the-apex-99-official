Planning to visit India for a medical consultation, treatment or health checkup? THE APEX 99 can assist with travel-related coordination and help you contact hospitals or healthcare providers for information and appointment requests.

  • Hospital and doctor appointment enquiry support

Important medical information: Treatment decisions, medical advice, clinical outcomes, hospital availability and prices are determined by the hospital and qualified medical professionals.
