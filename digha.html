PLACES WE PLAN TO COVER

🌊 New Digha Beach

Beach walk, sea view, sunset atmosphere and leisure time.

🌅 Old Digha

Explore the traditional Digha coastal area.

🐟 Digha Mohana

Experience the local fishing harbour and coastal life.

🏖️ Udaipur Beach

Peaceful coastal surroundings and photography stops.

🌴 Shankarpur

A quieter coastal destination near Digha.

🌿 Tajpur

Optional coastal sightseeing depending on time and route.

2 DAYS / 1 NIGHT ITINERARY

01 — KOLKATA → DIGHA

Early morning departure by private AC vehicle. Breakfast break on the way. Arrival at Digha, hotel check-in and lunch. Afternoon and evening sightseeing at New Digha and beach area. Dinner and overnight stay.

02 — DIGHA → UDAIPUR → SHANKARPUR → KOLKATA

Morning beach visit and breakfast. Visit Udaipur, Digha Mohana and Shankarpur according to the final schedule. Lunch and return journey to Kolkata.

ITINERARY NOTE: Exact sequence may change because of traffic, weather, road conditions and local restrictions.
